| 6 | Replaced the open-addressing hash workspace with a paged sparse array (`src/workspace.rs`): node ids split into fixed-size pages, a per-thread `page_table` of pointers (mostly `None`), pages drawn from a per-thread pool on first touch, and every page a query touched is zeroed and returned to the pool at that query's `reset()` (tracked via a `touched_pages` list, so reset cost is proportional to pages touched, not `n` or the pool's total size).
